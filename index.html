// archetypes

Where the policies differ: how the action head reads the scene

Every policy here turns cameras, an instruction and the robot's joint state into a chunk of future actions. They differ mostly in one connection: the path from the perception backbone into the action head. That connection is what makes some heads cheap to iterate and others free.
